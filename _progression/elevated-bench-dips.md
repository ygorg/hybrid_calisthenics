- Only lower yourself down until you feel a stretch at the shoulder to reduce the
  risk of injury. This exercise should not hurt!
- "Your elbows don\u2019t have to go straight behind you, but don\u2019t let them\
  \ go too far out to the side."
- "Distribute your weight evenly throughout your hands.\_"
- "Use a steady base for your feet as support. It shouldn\u2019t move around. "

tutorial:
- Sit at the edge of a sturdy bench or chair. Straighten your legs and place your
  heels on a sturdy surface at approximately the same height as your chair.
- Place your hands on the edge of the bench with your knuckles facing forward. Your
  toes should be pointed upward.
- Lift your hips off the bench and shift forward until your hips are past the seat.
- "Lean forward slightly and bend your elbows to slowly lower your hips toward the\
  \ floor. Stop when you feel a slight stretch at your shoulder.\_Pause for 1 second."
- "Press your hands into the bench to straighten your arms.\_Pause for 1 second."
- "Repeat steps 4 and 5 until you have completed your desired reps.\_"
