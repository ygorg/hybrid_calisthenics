- Taking our feet off the floor is a pretty big step, as it will require a lot more
  tension from our whole body to keep the position!
- Take extra care to keep your torso and legs aligned, with your feet firmly planted
  against the wall.

  back, and letting our core disengage. Be sure to keep tension by actively tensing
  your glutes and abdominal muscles, as well as pressing your arms into the floor!
- "A good mental cue to keep the right shoulder and arm position is to have your elbow\
  \ pits facing forward at about 45\xB0."
- "If you\u2019re dealing with wrist pain, performing these on your knuckles or something\
  \ like parallettes would be a great option."
- You might be tempted to hold your breath, but be sure to breathe throughout your
  holds!

short_desc:
- Time for our feet to come off the floor!
- Your arms and your body are straight, your feet are on the wall behind you. You
  should really start to feel the weight on your arms now! Your alignment comes into
  play here as well.

tutorial:
- Kneel on the floor with your knees together, with your feet close to a wall behind
  you.
- Lean forward and place your hands on the ground underneath your shoulders, fingers
  facing forward. Your arms should be and stay straight.
- Bring your shoulders down and forward.
- Straighten your legs, creating a straight line from head to knees.
- "Lift one foot off the floor and place it on the wall behind you at about elbow\
  \ height. When you\u2019re stable and pressed against the wall, do the same with\
  \ the second foot."
- Hold this position for time. See Standards.
