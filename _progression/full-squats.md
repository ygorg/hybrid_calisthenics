tutorial:
- Stand in a safe area with your feet shoulder width apart.
- Place your arms wherever they feel comfortable. Some extend their arms straight
  in front of them, and others place them across their chest.
- Squat down slowly until your hamstrings are pressed against your calves and you
  are unable to descend any further.
- Pause for 1 Second.
- Slowly come back up.
- Pause for 1 Second.
- Repeat 3-6.
