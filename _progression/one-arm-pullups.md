tutorial:
- Stand below a horizontal bar or rings
- 'Firmly grasp the bar with one hand. Your working arm should be straight. '
- Ascend slowly until your chin is above the bar or rings. Allow your free arm to
  move freely for balance.
- Pause for 1 Second.
- Slowly come back down.
- Pause for 1 Second.
- Repeat 3-6.
