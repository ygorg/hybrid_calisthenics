desc:
- After being well-versed with bilateral pushing, we can now officially begin assisted
  one-arm work.
- Having one arm further away gives more work to our working arm.
form:
- "Try to go straight up and down. If you find yourself leaning towards your assisting\
  \ arm, then simply bring it closer until you\u2019re able to go straight up and\
  \ down. Leaning makes the exercise easier."
- "Start with your assisting arm 2 palms\u2019 length away from your torso. Adjust\
  \ as needed."
- "Keep your body aligned. Don\u2019t sag!"
- Film yourself and watch back between sets to check your form. A mirror is also useful.
- Keep your feet together.
level:
- 2 Sets of 5 (Per Side)
- 2 Sets of 10 (Per Side)
- 2 Sets of 20 (Per Side)

name: Side-Staggered Pushups

prog_reg:
- 'To make this exercise harder: Moving your supporting arm further away makes this
  exercise harder. Doing this exercise with a straight assisting arm can be quite
  difficult. Be sure to go straight up and down. Moving side-to-side would turn this
  into a variation of Archer Pushups - which is explored later in our Pushup series.'
- "To make this exercise easier:  Moving your assisting hand closer to your torso\
  \ will make this exercise easier. Don\u2019t move it too close, or you\u2019ll just\
  \ be doing Full Pushups. If you\u2019re unable to maintain good form with your assisting\
  \ hand 1 palm length away from your torso, spend some more time on Narrow Pushups."
short_desc:
- Assisted one-arm pushing!
- "Because one arm is further away from the body, it is unable to assist as well as\
  \ the \u201Cworking\u201D arm. Keep the working arm\u2019s knuckles slightly below\
  \ its shoulders. Keep your assisting arm about two palms distance away from your\
  \ chest. Lower smoothly until your chest touches the floor. Pause briefly before\
  \ coming back up. The body should go straight up and down. If you find yourself\
  \ leaning towards your assisting arm, bring it closer until you can go straight\
  \ up and down."

tutorial:
- 'Kneel on the floor with your knees together. '
- "Lean forward and place your hands on the ground. Place your working hand under\
  \ your chest and your assisting arm 2 palms'\u2018 length away from your torso."
- Straighten your knees. Your arms should be straight and your body aligned from your
  head to knees.
- Descend straight down slowly until your chest gently touches the floor. Your working
  hand may brush your chest.
- Pause for 1 Second.
- Slowly come back up.
- Pause for 1 Second.
- Repeat 4-7.
