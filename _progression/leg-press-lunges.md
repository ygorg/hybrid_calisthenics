tutorial:
- Stand approximately 1 Foot from a raised surface.
- Slowly lunge forward until your hamstring touches your calf. Your torso should remain
  neutral and upright.
- Pause for 1 Second.
- Press down through your foot to slowly return to start.
- Repeat 2-4.
