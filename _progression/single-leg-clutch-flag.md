- "Stand with the back of your armpit against the pole.\_"
- "Grab the pole and slide your hand up until you reach sternum height.\_"
- "Brace your core and wedge the elbow of your opposite arm into your abdominals.\
  \ Grab the pole at about hip height.\_"
- Lift your feet off the ground and bend both knees.
- "Straighten your body until you are parallel to the ground.\_"
- Extend your top leg while keeping your bottom leg bent.
- Hold for as long as you can with good form.
- Switch sides and repeat.
