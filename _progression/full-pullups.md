tutorial:
- Stand below a horizontal bar or rings
- Firmly grasp the bar with both arms at shoulder width. Your entire body should be
  straight and off the ground.
- Ascend slowly until your chest gently touches the bar (or just below).
- Pause for 1 Second.
- Slowly come back down.
- Pause for 1 Second.
- Repeat 3-6.
