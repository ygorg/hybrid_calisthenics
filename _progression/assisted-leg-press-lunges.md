tutorial:
- "Find a raised surface and collect two objects to use as assistance. A pair of brooms,\
  \ mops, or rakes can be repurposed for support here. You can also use a tree, a\
  \ post, a pole, or even a wall for one-sided support.\_"
- Stand approximately 1 foot from your raised surface and hold your supports at arms
  length on either side of you. Ensure your supports are stable before moving on to
  Step 3.
- "Slowly lunge forward until your hamstring touches your calf. Try to keep your torso\
  \ neutral and upright. Allow your arms to bend naturally throughout the movement.\_"
- Pause for 1 Second.
- Press down through your foot to slowly return to start.
- Repeat 3-6.
