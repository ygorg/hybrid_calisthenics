tutorial:
- Sit at the edge of a sturdy bench or chair with your feet flat on the floor.
- Place your hands on the edge of the bench with your knuckles facing forward.
- Lift your hips off the bench and shift forward until your hips are past the seat.
- "Lean forward slightly and bend your elbows to slowly lower your hips toward the\
  \ floor. Stop when you feel a slight stretch at your shoulder.\_Pause for 1 second."
- "Press your hands into the bench to straighten your arms.\_Pause for 1 second."
- 'Repeat steps 4 and 5 until you have completed your desired reps. '
