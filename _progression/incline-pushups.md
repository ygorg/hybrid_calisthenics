short_desc:
- Introducing us to diagonal pushing!
- Start with something around sternum height. The lower the base, the harder this
  exercise becomes. Lower yourself until your chest gently touches the base. Your
  hands should be around sternum height by your ribs at the bottom of the movement.

tutorial:
- Stand facing your base - it should be approximately sternum height.
- Lean forward and place your hands on the base. Your arms should be straight.
- Descend slowly until your chest gently touches the base. Your hands may brush your
  chest.
- Pause for 1 Second.
- Slowly come back up.
- Pause for 1 Second.
- Repeat
