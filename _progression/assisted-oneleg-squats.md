tutorial:
- 'Stand in a safe area in front of Gymnastic Rings or a stable assisting platform. '
- Grab the assisting platform with your hands and raise one leg off the ground. Both
  legs should be straight.
- Squat down slowly on your back leg until your hamstrings are pressed against your
  calves and you are unable to descend any further.
- Pause for 1 Second.
- Slowly come back up.
- Pause for 1 Second.
- Repeat 3-6.
